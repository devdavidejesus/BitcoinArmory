Armory

An open-source Bitcoin wallet for keeping your savings offline.

Cold storage

Keep your private keys on a computer that never touches the internet, with paper backups that work forever.

Offline signing

Create transactions on an online watching-only wallet, then carry them on a USB key to the offline computer to sign.

Your own node

Armory talks to the Bitcoin network only through your Bitcoin Core node, so no third-party server sees your addresses.
