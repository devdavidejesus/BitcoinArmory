There is no official Armory email address. The best ways to reach the developers are:

 - **Bug reports and feature requests:** open an issue on the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/goatpig/BitcoinArmory/issues). Include your Armory version, operating system, the Armory log file and steps to reproduce the problem. If relevant, attach Bitcoin Core's `debug.log` as well.
 - **Questions and support:** the [Armory board on Bitcointalk.org](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?board=97.0).

## Security problems and impersonators

Please **do not** open a public issue for a security problem. The [Security page](/security/) explains how to report it privately, how Armory releases are signed, and how to recognize fake Armory apps and websites.

The Armory developers will never ask for your wallet files, passphrase, paper backup or private keys, and will never offer to "recover" funds for a fee.
